# Health Cards

A [Chickadee Bandit](https://chickadeebandit.com/app-library/health-cards) app.

Allergy and emergency health information for every family member. Generate a printable card to share with parents, caregivers, or schools.

## Features

- Allergies and emergency health information per family member.
- A printable card for caregivers, schools, or relatives.
- The adults keep the cards: every adult reads, adds to and changes every card. A child who is
  given the app reads their own card and changes nothing.
- A household can name a **care team**: a member group (a nanny who signs in as a guest, a
  grandparent) that reads every card marked "Share with the care team" — the allergies, the
  emergency medications, the contacts — and prints it, and sees nothing else. A card is kept with
  the adults until it is shared. The page offers the switch on your own card and on the children's
  cards; the hub itself lets any adult change who sees a card they can see. An admin chooses the group under
  **Care team** on the front page (the hub's member settings decide who is in it); until then the
  switch is not offered. Medications has its own Care team setting — choose the same group there.
  Each care-team member also needs Health Cards itself: a guest gets it by having Health Cards
  ticked in their invite; a child needs the app given to them in the hub's member settings, since
  it is for the adults by default. The care team never adds, edits or removes anything on a card.
- In a co-parenting space both parents keep a child's card: either parent creates it and adds
  to, changes or removes anything on it, whoever wrote the entry. Each parent's own card stays
  theirs — the other parent can read it but not change it or its entries. A child reads their
  own card and changes nothing.
- A share link (admins only) still gives a sitter without a login a read-only page of one card.
  In a co-parenting space a parent shares their own card or a child's, never the other parent's.

## Install

In your hub, go to **Apps → Install from URL** and paste:

```
https://github.com/firebirdsystems/chickadeebandit-health-cards/releases/latest/download/bundle.json
```
